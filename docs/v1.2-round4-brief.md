## 2. Defects

**D1 — Phases run before full membership exists; a generic window was
claimed for a specific entry (identity swap).** Launches and placement
phases interleave. WS3's phase placed 1 of its 2 saved windows; WS4's
placed 2 of 3; late windows were moved in after their workspace's
phase closed (a terminal into WS3 after WS9's phase; the log's only
hyprsession-cwd launch into WS4 after WS10's phase). In WS10's phase
the third window found and claimed was a generic `mike@omarchy:~`
terminal, while the window actually launched with the hyprsession
entry's argv landed on WS4. Proven at the argv level in the post-load
save (§3). Requirement: a workspace's placement must not claim a
window launched for a different entry while its own entry's launch is
outstanding; extend the launch-provenance binding already used for
resume entries, or complete all launches before any phase runs.

**D2 — Grown tree shapes diverge from the saved trees (WS4, WS10).**
WS4's tree came back transposed; WS10's came back a different shape
(§3). The steered schedule evidently did not govern the result.
Diagnosis is part of the deliverable: explain the mechanism in the
handoff (candidate causes to rule in or out: insertion in found order
rather than the schedule's order; the staging workspace being also a
target, so its tree grows among not-yet-moved staged windows;
split-direction vs leaf-centre handling) before or alongside the fix.

**D3 — Geometry read-back is per-phase and immediate.** WS3 read
"0 of 1" mid-assembly and finished geometrically exact (false alarm);
symmetrically, a workspace can read perfect at phase time and be
broken by a late arrival (false pass). Requirement: verify geometry
once, after ALL placements complete, against final state, reported
per workspace.

**Open question — pointer restore origin.** load.log's final warp is
to (1866, 121), immediately before the final focus. Mike reports he
did not start the load with the pointer in that quadrant. Establish
when the pre-load position is captured (it must be at process start,
before any dispatch) and log the captured origin and the restored
position.
